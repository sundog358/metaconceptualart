I am a contemporary American Artist working and living in Eugene, Oregon.  =
I have created the new field of contemporary Art known as Metaconceptual Ar=
t.

I have attached a few images of my Art for your viewing, however there are =
many others available upon request.



Two popular examples of Metaconceptual Art, which are ongoing through time =
and space as well, are titled "Art Market Reform" , and "Art Tax"



"Art Market Reform": Promoting Art Tax on the local, state, national, and i=
nternational levels to encourage and promote accountability, transparency, =
and regulation in the Art Market and Art World.



My Art business is named "Sun and Rain Works", and i am the Chief Executive=
 Artist at Sun and Rain Works



Part of the goal of "Art Market Reform" and "Art Tax", is to begin, encoura=
ge, and promote an open discussion regarding "Art Market Reform" and "Art T=
ax".

In May of 2009, i auctioned off Lot 16 of Sotheby's N.Y.C. photograph aucti=
on, Ansel Adams "San Francisco from Twin Peaks" as a performance act of Met=
aconceptual Art. There are 8 defining characteristics of Metaconceptual Art=
, which you can find on Google or Youtube, if you are interested in knowing=
 more about it.

I submitted a proposal to the White House in Washington D.C. outlining the =
proposal for "Art Market Reform" and "Art Tax", two days ago, and i am awai=
ting feedback from them, which is scheduled to arrive within the next 8 wee=
ks.

However, when we spoke on the telephone, they asked me to find local partie=
s and support for the proposal, and i also think State, National, and Inter=
national support are also essential for this art project to be a success, a=
nd this is the purpose of this discussion.

It was through my active participation with "The Art World", that i learned=
 firsthand of the system of "anonymous buyers", "anonymous sellers", "paper=
less trails", and otherwise complete opacity, lack of regulation, and lack =
of transparency, which is inherent in the Art Market.

With the enforcement of Art Tax, school books for poor children, food for h=
ungry people, and Art and Music programs could be funded from the revenues =
which are not otherwise collected.

Washington State estimates through their Department of Revenue that over 50=
0 million dollars is lost annually in Washington State alone due to the lac=
k of collection of their "Art use Tax".

Oregon, and the city of Eugene by parallel is very likely losing out on sim=
ilar amounts of Art Tax, which is a part of the overall goals of transparen=
cy, regulation, and accountability of Art Market Reform.

It is well documented in the New York Times, and many other media forums, t=
hat the Art Market is commonly used by criminals and others, to launder mon=
ey. The city of Eugene is not immune to the issues which plague the Art Wor=
ld, and the Art Market, both internationally, nationally, and locally.  Wha=
tever city, region, state or country you live in, we are all in this togeth=
er, the problem will not fix itself by ignoring it.  In the year 2013, all =
of our information and economies are interconnected and interdependent.  We=
 cannot let the financial meltdowns continue without getting to the root of=
 the problem.

The White House asked me to speak with people, groups, and organizations on=
 the local level, to proceed further, in the spirit of American Democracy a=
nd grassroots Reform.

I would like to encourage an open, fair and widespread discussion and parti=
cipation with "Art Market Reform" and "Art Tax" to help us collectively ame=
liorate our collective situation, and help to benefit all of civilization, =
both in our current situation, and for all ages to come.

All of Art History is contemporary Art first, and together we are helping t=
he Art Historians of the future to keep their employment, here and now. Art=
 Market Reform begins here, and now.

"Art Market Reform" and "Art Tax" are Metaconceptual Art works, which are o=
ngoing through time and space, and invite open participation and interactio=
n !

Here is a metaconceptual Art poem i wrote a few years ago, which i am publishing free to the world today, in honor of "Cyber Monday" 2011. if you are interested in it, or spreading it around, it is for the World and the Art World both, feel free.


"The Art World is Flat"

Colors explode over the canvass of life.

The banker childs' delight is the poor woman's strife.

Art dealers feed, until oceans run dry.

Monuments for Eternity, known by Third Eye.

Innovations, and derivations, from complexities womb,

perpetuate shadow play, in our cave like tomb.

WTF is Ansel Adams

Sotheby's Photographs, New York, sale N08533, lot 16, 2009.

 

Sun & Rain Works consigned an Ansel Adams photograph, "San Francisco from Twin Peaks", to Sotheby's Photographs in 2009, and designates that engagement as a work. The auction was Sotheby's ordinary business; the art is the designation.

The print was photographed in the studio on 8 August 2008 before it went out: recto, mount, verso, and a close-up of the verso credit block. The verso markings agree in four particulars with the public catalogue entry for lot 16 — the American Trust Company credit stamp, the 1-SF- negative-number series, the inked "Wells Fargo Bank" annotation over the struck-through article, and the handwritten title.

 

Documentation photograph, 8 August 2008. The rest of that session, and the storage-unit photographs made of the print, remain in the studio's private archive: they show an identifiable person and are held back pending consent.

 

This page is the reconstruction's, not 2009's.

Nothing in the surviving source folder shows that www.wtfisart.com carried a page about the consignment. The two were made in the same months and the studio describes them as companion works, but that pairing is the artist's account, recorded here as such. See about this reconstruction.
